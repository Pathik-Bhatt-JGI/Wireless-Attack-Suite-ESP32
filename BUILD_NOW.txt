METHOD 2: Manual Commands
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

2. Run: C:\Espressif\frameworks\esp-idf-v5.5.5\export.bat
3. Run: idf.py fullclean
4. Run: idf.py build
5. Run: idf.py -p COM17 flash
